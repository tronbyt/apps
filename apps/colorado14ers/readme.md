# Colorado14ers

Big mountains. Small display. Endless inspiration.

Colorado14ers brings a little Rocky Mountain adventure to your Tidbyt or compatible Tronbyt display.

Explore the Colorado peaks included in the app’s collection, keep track of the ones you’ve visited, and discover a featured mountain each time the app runs. Whether you’re remembering a hard-earned summit or dreaming about your next mountain morning, Colorado14ers keeps those high-country ambitions close at hand.

Part adventure journal, part mountain showcase, and part invitation to get outside—without the early alarm or the steep climb to your desk.

## Features

- Personal peak tracking — Mark the mountains you’ve visited and keep your adventures recorded in the app’s settings.
- Geographic context — See the locations of peaks in the app’s Colorado collection.
- A featured mountain each run — Learn about a randomly selected peak whenever a new presentation is generated.
- Three discovery modes — Feature any included peak, a visited peak, or an unvisited peak.
- Mountain-specific artwork — Bundled mountain outline images give the collection a little individual character.
- Metric and imperial options — Choose the measurement system you prefer.
- Multiple checklist sorting options — Organize the mountain selection list by name, range, elevation, hiking distance, or class.
- Adjustable scrolling — Choose slow, medium, or fast scrolling.
- Bundled mountain information — Peak records and artwork are included with the app.
- Three display formats — Support for standard 64 × 32, wide 128 × 64, and square 64 × 64 panels.

## Choose Your Mountain Mood

### Random 14er

Let the app choose from its included mountain collection.

A familiar favorite might appear—or a peak you haven’t thought much about yet. It’s a little high-country inspiration without needing to decide where to look first.

### Random Visited 14er

Turn your display into a reminder of the places you’ve already explored.

Mark your visited peaks, then use this mode to revisit those mountain memories: the early starts, the long climbs, and the views that made it all worthwhile.

### Random Unvisited 14er

Keep your next adventure in sight.

This mode draws from peaks you haven’t marked as visited, making your display a rotating collection of possibilities.

Your checklist determines what counts as visited or unvisited. The app does not automatically record hikes.

## More Than a Name on a Map

The app’s bundled mountain records include:

- Mountain name
- Mountain range
- Summit elevation
- Topographic prominence
- Latitude and longitude
- Hiking distance
- Hiking elevation gain
- Route class
- Mountain outline artwork
- Descriptive text

Together, these records give the app a mountain-by-mountain collection to explore—not just a set of dots.

The information is bundled with the app rather than retrieved as live trail or weather data.

## Keep Your Adventures Organized

The available sorting options help you browse the peak checklist in a way that suits you.

| Sort option | Organizes the list by |
| --- | --- |
| Mountain Name | Peak name |
| Range | Mountain range |
| Mountain Elevation | Summit elevation |
| Hiking Distance | Hiking distance stored in the dataset |
| Class | Class rating stored in the dataset |

Sorting helps organize your choices. The featured mountain is still selected randomly from the discovery mode you choose.

## Get Started

No API keys, subscriptions, or external data feeds are required.

1. Add Colorado14ers to your display.
2. Open the app’s settings.
3. Mark the peaks you’ve visited.
4. Choose whether to feature any peak, visited peaks, or unvisited peaks.
5. Select metric or imperial measurements.
6. Choose your preferred checklist sorting and scrolling speed.
7. Save your settings and enjoy a little mountain inspiration.

If you choose visited-only mode, make sure you’ve marked some peaks as visited. Likewise, leave some peaks unmarked to give unvisited-only mode a selection to draw from.

## Important Notes

- Visits are recorded manually in settings.
- Random selection can feature the same peak more than once.
- Replaying an existing rendered animation does not select a new mountain.
- The app tracks its included peak collection; do not assume it matches every published Colorado 14er list.
- Mountain descriptions and measurements come from the bundled dataset and are not live updates.
- Hiking distances, elevation gains, and class ratings are tied to the dataset’s entries and should not be assumed to describe every possible route.
- The app does not provide live weather, trail conditions, access restrictions, or closure information.
- Use it for inspiration and personal tracking—not navigation or safety decisions. Consult current, authoritative route and access information before a trip.
