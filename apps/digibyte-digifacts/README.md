# DigiFacts

Learn something new about [DigiByte](https://digibyte.org) every time this app comes round on your Tronbyt.

DigiFacts are bite-size facts about DigiByte - its history, technology, mining, security and community - written by the DigiByte community. The app opens with the DigiByte logo and the DigiFact's number, then the DigiFact scrolls up the display. If the DigiFact has a link, it ends with a QR code you can scan with your phone to learn more. It plays in full, however long the DigiFact is.

![DigiFacts on a 128x64 display](digifacts-readme.webp)

Works on 64x32, 64x64 and 128x64 displays.

## Settings

- **Language:** English, Deutsch, Español, Français, Italiano, Nederlands, Português or Русский. (DigiFacts are also available in Chinese, Hindi, Japanese and Korean, but the display fonts don't have those characters yet.)
- **Text size:** small, medium or large.
- **Show:** the DigiFact of the day, or a random DigiFact each time.
- **New DigiFact at:** the time the DigiFact of the day changes, in your time zone. 04:00 unless you choose another time.

## Help improve the DigiFacts

The DigiFacts live in the [digibyte-digifacts](https://github.com/DigiNode-Tools/digibyte-digifacts) repository on GitHub, and anyone can help:

- **Spotted a mistake, or a DigiFact that's out of date?** [Open an issue](https://github.com/DigiNode-Tools/digibyte-digifacts/issues) or make a pull request.
- **Translate:** improve the existing translations or add a new language - see the [translation guide](https://github.com/DigiNode-Tools/digibyte-digifacts/blob/main/translation-guide.md). You can read through the DigiFacts side by side with English on the [proofreading pages](https://digifacts.diginode.tools/proofread/).
- **New DigiFacts** are welcome too - see [how to contribute](https://github.com/DigiNode-Tools/digibyte-digifacts#contributing).

The app fetches the DigiFacts from [digifacts.diginode.tools](https://digifacts.diginode.tools/help/), so improvements show up on your display automatically.
