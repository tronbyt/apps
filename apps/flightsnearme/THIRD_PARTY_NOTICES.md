# Airline artwork

Full airline logos downloaded from Kiwi.com airline imagery at https://images.kiwi.com/airlines/64/{IATA}.png and resized to 12×12 PNGs. Sources are mapped in assets/airlines/sources.json. Airline logos and trademarks belong to their respective owners and are used to identify the operating airline. No logo downloads occur at runtime. Logos may depict an older brand revision.

The full PNGs are embedded in apps/flights.star for single-file uploads. Unknown and regional operators without a reliable brand mapping use an original unbranded tail silhouette. Tail artwork from Tronbyt has been removed from this app.

Alaska Airlines (ASA) uses the face emblem from its official favicon, https://www.alaskaair.com/favicon.ico, resized to 12×12 pixels.

Spotify app adapted from tronbyt/apps/apps/spotify/spotify.star, author gshepperd, Apache-2.0. Idle and paused defaults changed to false.
