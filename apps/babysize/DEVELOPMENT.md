# Weekly development sources

Original playful captions and factual summaries by [cptntrps](https://github.com/cptntrps), developed with
OpenAI Codex, based on **NHS Best Start in Life: week-by-week guide to pregnancy**.
Source pages checked on **2026-10-10**. NHS has not reviewed or endorsed this app.

These describe typical development around a gestational week; they are not
measurements or confirmation of an individual baby's development. Timing varies.
Week 40 highlights continuing movement rather than inventing a new milestone.
Before week 4 and from week 41 onward, the card displays a general timing message.
The app does not infer whether a baby has been born from the due date.

The panel presents one playful caption per week, with manually wrapped English and Portuguese
text. Portuguese accents are omitted for the small display font. The facts are
embedded in the generated app; no health information is sent to NHS or another API.

The jokes are shorthand: eavesdropping means hearing outside voices, not
understanding a conversation; the gourmet joke describes developing taste buds.
