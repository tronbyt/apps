# Practical tips and their evidence

Original panel copy by [cptntrps](https://github.com/cptntrps), developed with OpenAI
Codex. Guidance checked on **2026-10-10**. Sources are NHS, NIH, and CDC; those
organizations have not reviewed or endorsed this app.

A short **TRY THIS** card follows the playful development card. These are general
pregnancy suggestions, not individual medical advice or promises about a baby's
outcome. Food tips are for the pregnant parent. Adapt choices to allergies and
clinician advice. Individual supplement needs belong in prenatal care.

Week placement is editorial: nutrition matters throughout pregnancy, not only
when a corresponding milestone appears. Folic acid matters before conception and
early in pregnancy; do not wait for a card. Talking or singing is offered as a
connection activity, without a claim that it improves intelligence or language.
The dietary-variety tip makes no claim about refining taste buds or preventing
picky eating. Nutrient roles and absorption mechanisms are established knowledge;
the app does not describe every meal suggestion as a tested clinical intervention.

The 15-second cycle is week (3s), size (3s), playful development (4s), then tip (5s).
The tip header credits its main source. Full explanations and supporting links
follow. Before week 4 and from week 41 onward, the tip is a prenatal-care prompt.

## variety

**English:** VARY THE MENU / FOR A MIX / OF NUTRIENTS

**Portuguese:** VARIE O PRATO / PARA COMBINAR / NUTRIENTES

A varied, balanced diet supplies nutrients needed during pregnancy and supports growth. This does not claim to improve taste buds or prevent picky eating.

Sources: [source 1](https://www.nhs.uk/pregnancy/keeping-well/have-a-healthy-diet/).

## calcium

**English:** PASTEURIZED / YOGURT FOR / BONE CALCIUM

**Portuguese:** IOGURTE / PASTEURIZADO: / CALCIO P/ OSSOS

Yogurt supplies calcium, which is needed for bones and teeth. Choose pasteurized yogurt; calcium-fortified dairy alternatives are another option. This is food guidance, not a recommendation to add a calcium supplement.

Sources: [source 1](https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/Calcium-Consumer/), [source 2](https://www.nhs.uk/pregnancy/keeping-well/have-a-healthy-diet/), [source 3](https://www.nhs.uk/pregnancy/keeping-well/foods-to-avoid/).

## iron

**English:** BEANS + PEPPERS / FOR BETTER / IRON ABSORPTION

**Portuguese:** FEIJAO+PIMENTAO / AJUDAM A / ABSORVER FERRO

Beans provide nonheme iron. Vitamin C in sweet peppers helps the body absorb plant iron; iron is used to make the blood proteins that carry oxygen. This pairing illustrates that established mechanism, not a trial of this exact meal or a treatment for anemia.

Sources: [source 1](https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/Iron-Consumer/).

## choline

**English:** COOKED EGGS / ADD CHOLINE / FOR BABY GROWTH

**Portuguese:** OVOS COZIDOS / DAO COLINA / PARA CRESCER

Eggs supply choline, which is needed for fetal growth and central nervous-system development. Cook eggs until both whites and yolks are firm. This does not claim that extra choline boosts intelligence or prescribe supplements.

Sources: [source 1](https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/Pregnancy-HealthProfessional/), [source 2](https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/Choline-Consumer/), [source 3](https://www.nhs.uk/pregnancy/keeping-well/have-a-healthy-diet/).

## fibre

**English:** OATS ADD / FIBRE TO HELP / KEEP YOU GOING

**Portuguese:** AVEIA DA FIBRAS / PARA AJUDAR / O INTESTINO

Higher-fibre foods such as oats help digestion and can help prevent constipation. This tip benefits the pregnant parent's comfort; it does not claim a fetal developmental effect.

Sources: [source 1](https://www.nhs.uk/pregnancy/keeping-well/have-a-healthy-diet/).

## voice

**English:** CHAT OR SING / BABY CAN HEAR / YOUR VOICE

**Portuguese:** CONVERSE, CANTE / BEBE JA OUVE / SUA VOZ

The NHS describes hearing outside voices around week 21 and recognizing voices around week 31, and suggests talking or singing. This is a connection activity, not a proven intelligence or language boost. It needs no belly headphones or loud sound.

Sources: [source 1](https://www.nhs.uk/best-start-in-life/pregnancy/week-by-week-guide-to-pregnancy/2nd-trimester/week-21/), [source 2](https://www.nhs.uk/best-start-in-life/pregnancy/week-by-week-guide-to-pregnancy/3rd-trimester/week-31/).

## prenatal

**English:** BRING QUESTIONS / TO YOUR NEXT / PRENATAL VISIT

**Portuguese:** LEVE DUVIDAS / PARA A PROXIMA / CONSULTA

Early, regular prenatal care helps identify concerns and tailor care. Visits include discussion of questions about the pregnancy. This remains useful at any stage.

Sources: [source 1](https://www.nichd.nih.gov/health/topics/pregnancy/conditioninfo/prenatal-care).

## folic

**English:** CHECK FOLIC / ACID IN YOUR / PRENATAL PLAN

**Portuguese:** CONFIRA ACIDO / FOLICO NO SEU / PRE-NATAL

Folic acid before and during early pregnancy helps prevent neural-tube defects. Do not wait for this card or a particular week to discuss it. Follow prenatal guidance from your clinician; the app does not set an individual dose or replace supplementation with food.

Sources: [source 1](https://www.cdc.gov/folic-acid/about/index.html).

## produce

**English:** WASH FRUIT / AND VEG WELL / FOR FOOD SAFETY

**Portuguese:** LAVE BEM / FRUTAS E / VERDURAS

Careful washing removes soil from produce and reduces exposure to foodborne hazards. This is food-safety advice, not a milestone-specific intervention.

Sources: [source 1](https://www.nhs.uk/pregnancy/keeping-well/have-a-healthy-diet/).
