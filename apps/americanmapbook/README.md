# American Mapbook

Every adventure leaves a memory. Give yours a place to shine.

American Mapbook turns your Tidbyt or compatible Tronbyt display into a colorful celebration of the places you’ve explored across the United States. From magnificent national parks and historic state capitols to presidential libraries and World Heritage Sites, your travels become a living little map of discovery.

Watch the country take shape, see destinations appear, and enjoy the satisfying glow of all the places you’ve already checked off. It’s part travel journal, part adventure checklist, and part daily inspiration for wherever the road takes you next.

## Display Previews

American Mapbook supports three display formats, with layouts that adapt to your device.

### Standard Display — 64 × 32

A pocket-sized portrait of your American adventures. The standard layout brings the map, destination markers, and Alaska and Hawaii insets together on a classic 64 × 32 display.

![American Mapbook on a standard 64 × 32 display](americanmapbook.webp)

### Wide 2× Display — 128 × 64

A bigger canvas for a country full of possibilities. The higher-resolution wide version gives the map more room, with larger Alaska and Hawaii insets and a larger count font.

Destination markers remain one pixel each, keeping individual locations compact.

![American Mapbook on a wide 128 × 64 display](americanmapbook@2x.webp)

### Square Display — 64 × 64

Your adventures, framed a little differently. The square layout preserves the mainland map’s wide proportions rather than stretching it vertically, with Alaska and Hawaii positioned as accompanying insets.

![American Mapbook on a square 64 × 64 display](americanmapbook@64x64.webp)

## Choose Your Adventure

Select one of four tracking categories and mark the destinations you’ve visited.

### National Parks

Celebrate the landscapes that made you stop, stare, and reach for your camera. Build your own visual record of national park adventures, one unforgettable destination at a time.

### Presidential Libraries

Trace journeys through American leadership and history by tracking visits to the presidential libraries included in the app.

### State Capitols

Turn your travels into a coast-to-coast capitol quest. Mark the state capitols you’ve visited and watch your personal map of American civic history grow.

### World Heritage Sites

Track visits to the U.S. World Heritage Sites included in the app—from remarkable natural places to landmarks of cultural significance.

The map displays one selected category at a time, keeping each collection of adventures easy to follow.

## Features

- Animated map drawing — Watch the map outline appear first, followed by unvisited destinations and then the places you’ve explored.
- Four tracking categories — Choose National Parks, Presidential Libraries, State Capitols, or World Heritage Sites.
- Personal visit checklists — Mark visited locations directly in the app’s settings.
- Color-coded destinations — Tell visited and unvisited locations apart at a glance.
- Customizable colors — Set separate colors for the map outline, unvisited locations, and visited locations.
- Optional visited counter — Display a running count as visited locations appear.
- Alaska and Hawaii insets — Keep adventures beyond the contiguous United States in the picture.
- Visited-location priority — Visited markers are drawn last, helping them remain visible when locations share a pixel.
- A moment to admire your progress — The completed map holds before the animation begins again.
- On-device instructions — Enable scrolling help whenever you need a quick refresher.
- Three display formats — Support for standard 64 × 32, wide 128 × 64, and square 64 × 64 panels.

## Get Started

No external status feed, scripts, or API keys are required. Your map is built from the app’s included location data and the places you select in settings.

1. Add American Mapbook to your display.
2. Open the app’s settings.
3. Choose a category from the “Tracking” menu.
4. Use the generated checklist to mark the locations you’ve visited.
5. Enable “Count” if you’d like to display your visited total.
6. Customize the map and marker colors—or enjoy the defaults.
7. Save your settings and watch your adventures light up.

Need a reminder later? Enable “Show Instructions” for scrolling help on your display, then turn it off to return to your map.

## Available Settings

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Show Instructions | Displays scrolling help instead of your map. |
| Count | Shows the number of visited locations in the selected category. |
| Map | Sets the map outline color. |
| Unvisited | Sets the color of locations you haven’t marked as visited. |
| Visited | Sets the color of locations you’ve marked as visited. |
| Tracking | Chooses National Parks, Presidential Libraries, State Capitols, or World Heritage Sites. |
| Location checklist | Marks individual destinations as visited for the selected category. |

## How to Read Your Map

The animation builds your map in three stages:

1. The map outline appears, including Alaska and Hawaii insets.
2. Unvisited destinations are added.
3. Visited destinations light up in your chosen color.

If the counter is enabled, it increases as visited destinations appear. The finished map then pauses for approximately 7.5 seconds so you can take in your progress.

### Why the Count May Be Higher Than the Number of Dots

A big country has to fit into a small number of pixels. Nearby destinations can land on the same pixel, especially on smaller displays.

The counter tracks visited location entries, not distinct visible dots. Two visited destinations sharing a pixel still count as two visits checked off your list.

Visited destinations are drawn after unvisited ones, so a visited marker takes visual priority when they overlap.

## Notes

- Visits are marked manually in settings; the app does not automatically track your location or travel history.
- Available destinations come from the app’s bundled location lists.
- The map is a compact visualization, not a navigation tool.
- Alaska and Hawaii use separate inset scales and positions.
- Location markers remain one pixel on all supported display formats.
- Entries outside the supported mainland, Alaska, and Hawaii map groups are not plotted or included in the displayed count.
