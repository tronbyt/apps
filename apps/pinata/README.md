# Pinata

A colorful pinata swings on its rope under party bunting while a striped stick swings in
from either side to smack it. Hits knock off confetti and sometimes chunks of the pinata.
Sometimes it bursts, the bottom shatters, and wrapped candies, gumballs and lollipops spill
onto the grass.

Pinatas: classic 7-point star, donkey, llama, fish, ice cream cone and cactus. Colors
shift between plays.

## Settings

- **Pinata**: a specific shape, or Random for a new one each time.
- **Burst open**: Sometimes (default), Every time, or Never.

Supports 128x64 (2x) displays with smoother outlines and crepe-paper fringe detail.
