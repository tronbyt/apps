Luke's Split Flap Clock

Split flap clock that holds

A mechanical split flap board that flips once and then holds still until the next rotation. Clock layouts or a custom message, six themes, color cards, and four reveal patterns. A 64x64 square panel gets a six-row board instead of three, so the clock sits centred and a message runs to twice the length.
