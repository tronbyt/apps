WiFi QR Code

Creates a WiFi QR code

This app creates a scannable WiFi QR code. It is not compatible with Enterprise networks. Since there are display limitations with the Tidbyt, not all networks will be able to be encoded. Simply scan the QR code and your phone will join the WiFi network.
