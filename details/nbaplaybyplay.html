NBA Play By Play

Follow your NBA team live

Live game state for your favorite NBA team, sibling of NFL Play By Play. During games it shows the quarter and clock with the score on team-color bands, and the last play scrolls by when ESPN reports one. On game day it counts down to tipoff with both teams' records; the rest of the week it shows your team's record with division standings scrolling underneath. Works with no setup (it defaults to the Knicks) - pick your team in the settings. Uses ESPN's public scoreboard, no API key needed.
