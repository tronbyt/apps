LA Street Sweep

Next street sweep countdown

Counts down to your next Los Angeles street sweeping day with a cute side-view sweeper that closes in as the deadline nears. Set your weekday, week-pattern (1st & 3rd / 2nd & 4th / every week / non-posted) and time per side of the street. Find your days at streets.lacity.gov/services/street-sweeping. Data comes from the City of LA's public sweep-day calendars (holidays already excluded).

By Ryan Taylor (@ryantaylor16)
