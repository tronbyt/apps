Umbrel Updates

Umbrel and app updates

Shows when a new version of umbrelOS is available, and which apps on your Umbrel home server have an update, from the official Umbrel App Store and any community app stores. By default it only appears when there are updates. Needs umbrelOS 2.0 or later, and a Tronbyt server that can reach your Umbrel.
