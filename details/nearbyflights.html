Nearby Flights

Live ADS-B flights near you

Displays live flight data from the OpenSky Network for aircraft within a configurable radius of your location. Shows callsign, heading, altitude, speed, and origin country for up to 8 nearby planes. Configure by US ZIP code or lat/lon coordinates.
