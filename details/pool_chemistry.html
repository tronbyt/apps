Pool Chemistry

Water Guru pool chemistry via Home Assistant

Displays pH, free chlorine, temperature, alkalinity, CYA, and calcium hardness from a Water Guru Pro pool sensor via Home Assistant. Color-coded: green = good, orange = borderline, red = needs attention. Cycles through an overview and six individual detail slides every 3 seconds.
