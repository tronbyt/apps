Nightscout

Shows Nightscout CGM Data

Displays Continuous Glucose Monitoring (CGM) blood sugar data (BG, Trend, Delta, IOB, COB) from Nightscout. Will display blood sugar as mg/dL or mmol/L. Optionally display historical readings on a graph. Also a clock. For support, join the Nightscout for Tidbyt Facebook group. (v2.6.2).

By Paul Murphy, Jason Hanson, Jeremy Tavener
