Airplanes Live Overhead

Closest aircraft overhead

Shows the single closest aircraft to your location using an airplanes.live ADS-B feed (no API key): callsign, type, altitude, distance, bearing, speed, and a plane icon rotated to its heading. Surfaces emergency squawks (7500/7600/7700) first. Renders at 64x32 and 128x64. Data from airplanes.live; for personal, non-commercial use.
